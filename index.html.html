How I approach communication

The method.

Good communication is more than putting information out. It is making sure the right people can understand it, see its relevance and know what to do with it.

Understand the audience.＋
Find the clearest story.＋
Choose the right format.＋
Make the message useful and accessible.＋
Coordinate, deliver and learn.＋
